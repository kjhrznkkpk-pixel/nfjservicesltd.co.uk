Our Services

NFJ Services LTD provides practical, professional installation, cabling, WiFi, CCTV and maintenance services for homes, small businesses and commercial premises across Cheshire, Lancashire and Merseyside.

Commercial & Domestic Network Cabling

Ethernet and data cabling for homes, offices, garden rooms, TV areas and business premises, installed neatly for fast and reliable connectivity.

WiFi & Mesh WiFi Installation

Better WiFi coverage for weak signal areas, including mesh WiFi, access points and whole-home or business network setup.

Network Cabinet Tidy & Fault Finding

We trace, label, test and tidy network cables, routers, switches and patch panels. We can also replace damaged RJ45 plugs or sockets where needed.

CCTV Installation

CCTV camera installation and setup for homes and businesses, with neat cabling, sensible camera positioning and reliable system setup.

TV Mounting & Multi-Room AV

TV wall mounting, cable hiding, media wall cabling and multi-room audio visual setup for cleaner, easier-to-use entertainment spaces.

Smart Home & Automation

Smart doorbells, cameras, speakers, lighting support, connected devices and home automation setup explained in plain English.

Fibre Optic Cabling

Fibre cabling support for fast, reliable links between buildings, offices, cabinets and network equipment where standard cabling is not the best option.

Garden Room Connectivity

Network, WiFi, smart tech and installation support for garden rooms, offices and outbuildings, helping you get a reliable connection away from the main house.

Starlink Installation & Integration

Starlink positioning, cable routing and network integration for homes, rural properties, businesses, outbuildings and hard-to-reach locations.

Electrical & Maintenance Support

Small electrical jobs, practical property maintenance and general installation support completed cleanly and professionally.

Areas We Cover

We cover Cheshire, Lancashire and Merseyside, including Warrington, Runcorn, Widnes, Chester, Ellesmere Port, Liverpool, St Helens, Southport, Preston, Chorley, Leyland, Wigan, Skelmersdale and surrounding areas.
