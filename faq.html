WiFi, Routers & Internet

What is mesh WiFi in simple terms?

Mesh WiFi uses more than one WiFi box around your home or business.

Instead of all your WiFi coming from one router, extra WiFi boxes help spread the signal into more rooms.

Think of it like adding extra speakers around a house so the sound reaches everywhere. Mesh does that for WiFi.
Why is my WiFi good downstairs but bad upstairs?

This is usually because the WiFi signal is struggling to travel through walls, ceilings, furniture or distance.

The router may also be in a poor place, such as behind a TV, inside a cupboard or at one end of the house.

Will better WiFi make my internet faster?

It can make your internet feel much faster in rooms where the WiFi is currently weak.

It cannot make your broadband package faster than what you pay your provider for, but it can help you get a better connection around the property.

Why is the router from my internet provider not always good enough?

Most provider routers are made to be basic and cheap enough for average use.

They can struggle in larger homes, older houses with thick walls, busy homes with lots of devices, or places with CCTV, smart TVs, games consoles and home working equipment.

A better router or WiFi system can give stronger coverage and a more stable connection.

What is a WiFi booster?

A WiFi booster tries to repeat your existing WiFi signal.

They can help in some homes, but if the booster is repeating a weak signal, the result can still be poor.

A proper WiFi access point or mesh system is usually a better long-term fix.
Can you get WiFi into a garden room?

Yes. The best way is usually to run a proper cable from the house to the garden room, then fit a WiFi point inside the garden room.

This is normally much better than trying to stretch the house WiFi through walls and across the garden.

What is Starlink?

Starlink is internet that comes from satellites instead of a normal phone line or fibre cable.

It can be useful in places where normal broadband is slow or not available.

We can help with positioning, cable routing and connecting Starlink into your home or business network.

Network Cables

What is network cabling?

Network cabling is internet cable fitted around your home or business.

It lets devices connect with a cable instead of relying on WiFi. This is often faster and more reliable.

Is a cable better than WiFi?

For fixed devices, yes. A cable is usually better for things that do not move.

This includes smart TVs, games consoles, office computers, CCTV recorders, WiFi access points and media equipment.

Simple answer: phones and tablets are fine on WiFi. TVs, offices and CCTV are often better on cable.
What is Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6A, Cat7 and Cat8?

These are different types of internet cable. The higher numbers are usually made for better performance, but you do not always need the highest one.

  • Cat5e: Good for many basic jobs.
  • Cat6: A strong choice for most homes and small businesses.
  • Cat6A: Better for longer runs and faster networks.
  • Cat7: Not usually needed for normal home installs.
  • Cat8: Usually for specialist equipment, not normal homes.
For most customers, Cat6 or Cat6A is the sensible choice.

CCTV, TVs & Smart Tech

Yes. We can install CCTV cameras for homes and businesses.

We can help with where to place cameras, how to run the cables neatly and how to get the system set up.

Is wired CCTV better than wireless CCTV?

Wired CCTV is usually more reliable because it does not depend on WiFi signal.

Wireless cameras can work well in some places, but wired cameras are normally better for long-term reliability.

Can you mount my TV on the wall?

Yes. We can help mount TVs and make the setup look neater.

We can also help with cable management, media wall cabling and planning where equipment should go.

Can you hide TV wires?

In many cases, yes. The best method depends on the wall, the room and what equipment is being connected.

We can advise what is possible and what will give the cleanest finish.

What is a smart home device?

A smart home device is something that connects to your phone, WiFi or home network.

This could be a camera, doorbell, light, speaker, thermostat, smart plug or other connected device.

Can you set up smart home devices for me?

Yes. We can help install and set up smart devices so they are connected properly and easier to use.
